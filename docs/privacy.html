Crystal Bowl Scanner: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What the app collects

The app takes photos and 3D measurements of samples you scan. These stay on your iPhone. The app has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks only for the drive.file permission. With it the app can see and change only the files it creates itself in your Drive (your scan packages). It cannot read, list or change any other file in your Drive. Your Google sign-in token is kept in the iPhone's Keychain and is used only to upload your scans.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or sent anywhere other than your own Google Drive, and only when you turn that on.

Removing your data

Delete a scan in the app to remove it from the phone. Disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
