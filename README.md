# Avradip Ghosh

**Digital Rock Simulation Scientist**  
**Pore-scale fluid flow · Porous-media transport · Scientific computing**

I work on simulations of fluid flow through digital rock. My focus is understanding how pore geometry, connectivity and boundary conditions influence flow and transport properties, including permeability.

I connect three-dimensional rock structures to numerical models, inspect the resulting flow behavior, and examine whether the calculation supports the physical interpretation.

## Scientific focus

| Area | The questions I work on |
| --- | --- |
| Digital rock models | How can a three-dimensional image represent the pore space and solid structure needed for simulation? |
| Pore connectivity | Which pathways connect across the modeled domain, and which pores remain isolated? |
| Fluid flow | How does fluid move through the resolved pore structure under specified boundary conditions? |
| Permeability and transport | How do geometry, flow direction and the simulated response relate to effective transport properties? |
| Numerical reliability | How sensitive is a result to resolution, domain size, segmentation and solver convergence? |

## How I approach a simulation

I start with the geometry and the physical question. I make the model assumptions, units and boundary conditions explicit, then examine the numerical result in that context.

I care about mass conservation, convergence, resolution dependence and reproducibility. Pore connectivity helps describe the structure, but permeability requires a flow calculation. Visualization helps inspect a result, while the numerical fields and checks support its interpretation.

## Technical toolkit

- **Scientific computing:** Python, C++, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and Jupyter.
- **Digital rock analysis:** three-dimensional image and volume handling, pore geometry, segmentation and connectivity analysis.
- **Simulation:** numerical modeling of fluid flow and transport through porous media.
