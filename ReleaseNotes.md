# Release notes

Newest first. The first section's bullet points are shown in the app's update banner,
so write them for the person using the app.

## 1.5
- Fix the map any time: drag and turn rooms, rename them, and put rooms on the map that were scanned on their own or only photographed
- Add rooms you missed, or rescan a room that didn't come out well: the app finds your earlier scan so the new one joins the same map
- Every scan is kept in a history: view old scans in AR, put a room back the way an earlier scan saw it, re-read old scans with new features
- Name rooms later: tap "Name later" while scanning; unnamed rooms get a "Name me" badge and name suggestions
- Plans and designs remember who made them (Claude or ChatGPT); all versions are kept and "Improve" makes a better one
- Nested storage: desk › drawer › tray as deep as you like, and "Photo inside this spot" puts what it finds right there
- Kitchen planner: work triangle, the five kitchen zones, use-by dates and "use soon", and an AI kitchen organiser
- Beyond furniture: wall pictures and decor, organisers and helpful gadgets (visual timers, key finders, charging station…)
- New Shopping tab: everything the designer and kitchen organiser suggest buying, with rough prices

## 1.4
- Design studio for every room: each piece of scanned furniture is separate, drag it, turn it, resize it
- Add furniture and decor from a catalog (L-shaped sofa, beds, desks, rugs, plants, lamps) and wall pieces (art, mirrors, shelves, TV)
- Talk to the designer (Claude or ChatGPT): themes, layouts, furniture and wall ideas; its layouts open as new designs
- Feng Shui (bagua map, chi flow) and Vastu (compass zones from your scan's real north) shown on the plan
- Walkway check: overlapping furniture and tight paths are marked
- See any design in 3D and life-size in AR, and get a realistic picture of how your room will look (needs a ChatGPT key)
- "Which design is best?": the designer ranks your ideas and explains why
- Arranged the room for real? Tap "I've arranged the room like this" and the storage spots move with it

## 1.3
- Scan the whole place at once: walk room by room with LiDAR, name each room, and the app builds one map of the whole home with every room in its real position
- Geotagged automatically: the phone's GPS and compass place the home, each room and roughly each thing on the map, with the street address
- Each place has its own page: map with pins, the whole place in 3D and in AR, and a floor plan with a north arrow
- The app knows where you are: "You're at Home" and that place comes first; new photos are geotagged
- Google Drive gets each place's map file (place.geojson, opens in Google Earth), floor plan and 3D scan

## 1.2
- ChatGPT works too: add a ChatGPT key next to your Claude key and choose who answers, or let one back up the other automatically
- Your Claude key, ChatGPT key and Google Drive sign-in stay through every update, and come back after a reinstall or on a new iPhone (iCloud Keychain)
- Settings shows which keys are saved and has a Test button for each

## 1.1
- AR: tap "Tag & measure what I see" and labels with names, sizes and where each thing goes appear on the real things
- Place storage spots where they really are in AR; arcs in the room show where to carry each thing
- The room remembers its AR labels: open AR again and they come back in the same places
- Things are measured automatically: LiDAR sizes in AR (using Crystal Bowl's measuring maths), Claude's estimate from photos
- Floor plan: a top-down map of each room with numbered things and arrows to their homes
- Automatic Google Drive backup: the whole inventory (JSON and spreadsheet), floor plans, LiDAR room scans and pictures, in the background too

## 1.0
- Map your spaces: places (home, office), rooms or smaller segments, and their storage spots
- Scan with photos or a short walkthrough video: Claude names and tags every thing, spots what's out of place and suggests where it lives
- Scan the room shape with LiDAR: real size, walls and furniture become storage spots on the map
- Rough 3D map: drag to turn, tap a dot, arcs show where each thing goes
- Plans made for ADHD: pick your energy and minutes, get tiny steps grouped into carry trips
- Focus mode: one step, a gentle timer, Done / Skip / "Too big?" to split it smaller
- Find anything: search, or ask "where did I put my passport?"
